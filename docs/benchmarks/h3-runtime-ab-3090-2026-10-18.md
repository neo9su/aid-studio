# H3 768P runtime A/B on RTX 3090 — 2026-10-18

## Decision

Scheduler job `56` (`h3-runtime-ab-768p-valid-multiref-v3`) passed the
performance, media-integrity and sampled visual-quality gates. After explicit
administrator confirmation, the standard H3 production service was switched
to `torch 2.9.1 + CUDA 13.0` on 2026-10-18. Scheduler job `58`
(`h3-prod-cu130-768p-valid-multiref-v1`) then passed the first-production-run
gate with the same two ordered references.

After the A/B run, scheduler job `57` restored and verified the independent
GPU1 `h3-single-fast` lane. Its cold 480P FastH3 task completed in 605.4
seconds, the result fully decoded as 832×480 H.264 + AAC, and GPU1 entered the
six-hour `h3-single-fast` warm state. The production switch changed only the
GPU0 standard H3 Python runtime; GPU1 stayed warm and its profile was not
changed.

The candidate is not bit-exact. It preserves the production model, workflow,
20-step dense PyTorch attention path and reference inputs, but some seeds take
a different stochastic trajectory. Rollback must remain immediate.

## Media and quality gates

All six files passed full ffmpeg video+audio decode. Every output is 1344×768,
124 frames at 24 fps, 5.167 seconds, with AAC 32 kHz stereo audio. Freeze and
silence scans found no events.

The first, quarter, middle, three-quarter and final frames were reviewed for
all outputs. Both adult Chinese identities and their required clothing were
retained: the man has black wavy hair, dark suit, white shirt and dark tie; the
woman has long black hair, a light floral blouse and black trousers. Gaze and
eye alignment were coherent, hands and limbs had no obvious sampled-frame
defect, the interaction matched the prompt, and no severe sampled-frame
flicker appeared.

Decoded outputs are not bit-exact. Same-seed cross-runtime SSIM/PSNR was
`0.6793/17.06 dB` (cold), `0.9477/30.42 dB` (warm 1) and `0.7852/19.19 dB`
(warm 2). This is accepted as an explainable runtime trajectory change for
this six-clip sample, not proof that all prompts are equivalent. Monitor the
first production batch and rollback on a material face, gaze, hand, identity,
motion or audio regression.
